I'm a PhD student in Public Policy Analysis and Evaluation at Sapienza University of Rome.
I work in applied economics, in the areas of development economics, economic history and policy evaluation.
During my PhD I was a visiting PhD student at the Department of Geography and Environment of the London School of Economics in 2025, and at the Department of Economics of the University of Southern Denmark in 2025–2026.

## My research
My research investigates how policies should be designed to succeed in the areas they target.
I address this question through historical interventions, whose long time horizons and rich institutional variation provide a rigorous setting that helps identify what makes such policies effective.
My work thus lies at the intersection of economic history and development economics: development economics provides the question, and economic history the means to answer it, because the effects of these policies take decades to appear and recent programmes are too young to show them.

My work so far studies one case, the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno, through which Italy financed the development of its southern regions from 1950 to 1992.
My job market paper studies how the programme's support to industry was organised and what that meant for jobs.
A second paper turns to its public works and the movements of people, and a third describes the programme as a whole and what can be learned from it.
