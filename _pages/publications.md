## Work in progress

**Designing and Evaluating Place-Based Industrial Policy: The Cassa per il Mezzogiorno, 1950–1992**<br>
We review how industrial policy has been defined, why governments intervene and which tools each reason calls for. We then describe the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno along three dimensions of place-based policy design: where it intervened, what it offered, and how its support reached municipalities and firms.

**Top-Down or Bottom-Up? What the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno Reveals About Industrial Policy Design**<br>
*With Augusto Cerqua and Guido Pellegrini.*<br>
We compare two strategies of industrial development that ran side by side within the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno: concentrating investment in industrial consortia, and supporting firms spread across the territory. We find that the concentrated strategy raised industrial and manufacturing employment more, even at similar levels of funding.

**Reasons to Stay: Public Investment and Residential Mobility in Southern Italy**<br>
*With Giovanni Mellace and Paul Sharp.*<br>
We study how public investment in Southern Italy affected people's decisions to leave or move into a municipality. We find that public works reduced both departures and arrivals, while net migration barely changed.
